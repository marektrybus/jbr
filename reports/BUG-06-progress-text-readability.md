# BUG-06: Completed progress text intersects the blue bar on macOS Aqua

**Severity: Minor (visual usability).** At completion, black `Done` text is
centered over a thin blue progress bar that runs through the letters. The
submitter finds the result difficult to read. Loading and completion reporting
still work.

## Reproduction

1. On macOS with OpenJDK 21.0.13 and its default Aqua look and feel, launch:
   `"$JAVA_HOME/bin/java" suite/lib/Gallery.java 400`.
2. Wait until the scan finishes and the status reports 400 artworks.
3. Inspect the bottom-left progress bar: the filled blue bar crosses `Done`.

For a repeatable component capture, use the commands and diagnostic source in
the [evidence record](../evidence/manual-2026-11-08-startup/OBSERVATION.md).
The diagnostic invokes the real application entry point with 400 artworks.

## Expected and actual behavior

Expected: the required completion text `Done` remains clearly distinguishable
from the progress indicator.

Actual: black text is centered on a thin blue horizontal indicator running
through its letters. There are no overlapping `100%` and `Done` strings; only
`Done` is painted, over a full bar.

The PDF's **Expected behaviour → Scanning**, page 1, requires the bar to read
`Done` when complete. That functional condition passes. This report concerns
readability; the PDF supplies no explicit contrast threshold or visual-design
criterion. It does not require hiding or resetting a completed progress bar.

## Suspected root cause

Gallery enables `progress.setStringPainted(true)` in `buildUi` and later sets
the string to `Done`. In this environment, AquaProgressBarUI combines a thin
native-style blue indicator with centered black text inside a 146 x 20 logical
unit component. Their shared centerline produces the intersection. This appears
to be a look-and-feel presentation interaction; an underlying JDK defect has
not been established.

BUG-02 separately proves worker-thread progress mutations. These captures do
not establish that those mutations cause this stable completion appearance.

## Environment and limitations

Reproduced with macOS 26.7.2, OpenJDK 21.0.13 and AquaLookAndFeel on a 2x display.
Evidence is generated by Swing `printAll` on the EDT after normal main-entry
startup, rather than an OS screenshot. Other look and feels, platforms and native
screen compositing have not been verified. No automated legibility assertion,
contrast-compliance claim or application fix is included. Manual scenario: M-01.
